Botanical Engineering

Engineered for Longevity, Air Purity & Zero Stress

Drawing directly from NASA air purification studies and modern horticulture, our vertical wall systems are designed to thrive with minimal water, zero mess, and long-lasting vibrancy.

🚀

NASA-Inspired Air Purification

We curate specific foliage compositions proven to actively scrub airborne toxins (formaldehyde, benzene, particulate pollutants), providing natural oxygenation for indoor workspaces and residential lounges.

💧

Automated Micro-Drip Irrigation

Smart timed drip cycles deliver exact nutrient-infused moisture directly to the root zone, consuming up to 80% less water than traditional planter systems without manual watering hassles.

🌱

Specialized Soil-less Organic Media

Proprietary lightweight, pest-resistant soil-less growing mediums eliminate structural wall overload, foul odor, and waterlogging while providing high nutrient retention.

🔇

Acoustic & Thermal Buffering

Dense vertical foliage blocks direct sun radiation, dropping indoor ambient temperatures by 3°C–6°C and dampening ambient echo in open corporate atriums and high ceilings.
